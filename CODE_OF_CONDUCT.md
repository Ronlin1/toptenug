# TopTenUG Community Code of Conduct

TopTenUG welcomes contributors from different backgrounds, disciplines, communities, and levels of experience.

## Our standard

We expect project discussions to be:

- respectful and constructive;
- focused on evidence, methodology, design, and implementation rather than personal attacks;
- welcoming to newcomers and good-faith questions;
- careful when discussing real people, organizations, and communities represented in ranking data;
- transparent about uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and limitations.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in TopTenUG spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- unsupported allegations about ranked people or organizations;
- publishing private, leaked, or sensitive personal information;
- attempts to manipulate rankings through fabricated evidence, coordinated spam, or deceptive submissions;
- persistent disruption of technical or methodology discussions;
- impersonation or knowingly false identity claims.

## Ranking discussions

Disagreement with a ranking is welcome when it is evidence-based.

Please distinguish among:

- **data corrections** — a source value, identity, or factual record is wrong;
- **methodology criticism** — the algorithm, weighting, normalization, or eligibility rules should change;
- **preference** — a contributor personally values different criteria.

TopTenUG should respond to the first two through evidence and transparent methodology rather than personal argument.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project integrity.

When possible, moderation decisions should be proportionate, documented, and focused on restoring productive participation.

## Reporting

Use the repository's issue/discussion mechanisms for ordinary project concerns. For a sensitive moderation matter, contact the project maintainers through an appropriate private channel rather than publishing private details in a public issue.
